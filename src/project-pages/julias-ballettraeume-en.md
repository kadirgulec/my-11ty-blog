## Why this project

My daughters take ballet lessons at Julia's Ballettträume in Düren, and Julia
is a great teacher.

So I asked myself a simple question: how do more families find her? Parents
today look for a ballet school on their phone. If they don't find one there,
they never get as far as a first lesson. I wanted Julia to reach more children
and teach them ballet too — and a website is something I can build.

## What parents need

I thought about the questions parents ask before they sign up a child: Which
course fits my daughter's age? When are the lessons? Can we just try it first?

The site answers exactly these. The courses go from early ballet for toddlers to
classes for adults, the weekly timetable shows every lesson, and the first three
lessons are free. Parents ask for them with a short form, and Julia gets the
request by e-mail. Everything works on a phone, because that is where most
parents will open it.

## Keeping it easy for Julia

Julia teaches ballet. She shouldn't have to learn how to run a website. So I
keep the content up to date for her: a new timetable, a new performance, a
changed price — she sends me a message and I change it. If she ever wants to do
it herself, there is a simple editor and a short guide I wrote for her.

## Children's photos

A ballet school lives from its performances, and the best pictures show
children on stage. Before any photo went online, Julia agreed to publishing
pictures of the children with blurred faces. I blurred every face by hand —
also the children in the background — and checked each photo before it went on
the site. The map on the contact page also loads only when a visitor clicks on
it, so nothing is sent to Google before that.

## Going live

The site went live on 25 November 2026 at
[juliasballett.de](https://juliasballett.de), with its own domain and e-mail
address for the school. Now the part that I can't build starts: families
finding it, coming for a trial lesson, and staying.

## What I learned

- **Start from the visitor's questions.** Age, times, a free first lesson —
  the site is built around what parents want to know before they sign up.
- **Children's privacy comes before nice pictures.** Consent first, blurred
  faces always.
- **Small clients need small effort on their side.** A site the client never has
  to touch is often the better product.
- **A website is only the start.** It helps families find the school; the rest
  is up to Julia's teaching.
